## 3. Learning Outcomes per Topic

Verbatim completeness (exact lettering, no missing bullets) is NOT guaranteed for any topic — bullets below are those actually quoted from SOA syllabus documents unless marked otherwise.

### Topic 1 — Short-Term Insurance and Reinsurance Coverages (5–10%)
Objective: "understand and be able to apply the key features of insurance and reinsurance coverages." [OFFICIAL]
- Identify the types of coverage modifications for short-term insurance. [OFFICIAL]
- Perform calculations assessing the impact of coverage modifications. [OFFICIAL]
- Perform calculations of the loss elimination ratio and the effect of inflation on losses. [OFFICIAL]
- Identify the operation of basic forms of proportional and excess of loss reinsurance and understand their impact on reserving and pricing. [OFFICIAL]
- Determine the allocation of claim amounts paid by insurer and reinsurer under various forms of reinsurance. [OFFICIAL]
- [SECONDARY] Coverage modifications in study materials: deductibles (ordinary/franchise), policy limits, coinsurance, inflation; reinsurance forms: quota-share, surplus-share, per-risk / stop-loss excess of loss. Named subtypes NOT confirmed as verbatim syllabus text.

### Topic 2 — Severity, Frequency, and Aggregate Models (12.5–17.5%)
Objective: "understand the characteristics of and uses for commonly used severity, frequency, and aggregate models." [OFFICIAL]
- Calculate moments and percentiles; identify the role of scale and shape parameters in continuous models. [OFFICIAL]
- Recognize classes of distributions and their relationships; characterize distributions by existence of moments. [OFFICIAL]
- Calculate Value at Risk and Tail Value at Risk; determine whether a risk measure has certain desirable properties. [OFFICIAL]
- Define collective and individual risk models and calculate their mean and variance. [OFFICIAL]
- Use the log-normal or normal approximation for the aggregate distribution. [OFFICIAL]
- Calculate probabilities using the convolution method. [OFFICIAL]
- Calculate the expected payment for stop-loss insurance. [OFFICIAL]
- [SECONDARY] Loss Models §9.7 (impact of individual policy modifications — deductibles, limits, coinsurance — on aggregate payments) was ADDED to readings (Nov 2024).
- [SECONDARY] Distributions in scope via tables/manuals: (a,b,0) frequency class (Poisson, binomial, negative binomial, geometric); severity: exponential, gamma, Weibull, lognormal, Pareto; mixtures.
- [UNVERIFIED] (a,b,1) zero-truncated/zero-modified class — likely NOT on FAM (ASTAM material) but not established. Old STAM bullets (creating new distributions by scaling/powers/mixing) — [UNVERIFIED] for 2026.

### Topic 3 — Parametric Estimation (2.5–7.5%)
Objective (paraphrase): estimate parameters for parametric severity/frequency/aggregate models, primarily via **Maximum Likelihood Estimation**. [OFFICIAL]
- [SECONDARY] Method of moments also covered; percentile matching appears in study materials but is [UNVERIFIED] as a verbatim outcome.
- [UNVERIFIED] Goodness-of-fit testing claimed by one source (freefellow.org) — no official corroboration; historically ASTAM material. Treat with caution.
- Verbatim bullet list for this topic: **not established**. Censored/truncated-data and estimator-variance outcomes surfaced only in old Exam C/LTAM docs — [UNVERIFIED] for FAM and probably NOT included.

### Topic 4 — Introduction to Credibility (2.5–5%)
Objective: "understand the concepts of credibility and be able to apply certain types of credibility in some practical settings." [OFFICIAL]
- Scope: **limited fluctuation (classical) credibility ONLY**, including full and partial credibility criteria. [OFFICIAL scope; exact bullet phrasing UNVERIFIED]
- **Bühlmann, Bühlmann-Straub, Bayesian, empirical Bayes: NOT on FAM** — they are on ASTAM. [OFFICIAL by exclusion / SECONDARY]

### Topic 5 — Pricing and Reserving for Short-Term Insurance Coverages (10–15%)
Objective: "use basic methods to calculate premiums and reserves for short-term insurance coverages." [OFFICIAL]
Outcomes ([SECONDARY] — quoted from a Scribd copy of the official 2024-10 syllabus; lettering not independently verified):
- a) Estimate unpaid claims using **Expected Loss Ratio, Chain-Ladder (loss development), and Bornhuetter-Ferguson** methods
- b) Understand ratemaking objectives and ratemaking data
- c) Calculate ratemaking-data adjustments: **loss development, trend, on-level (current rate level) premium**
- d) Understand how **expenses and profit & contingencies loading** are used in ratemaking
- e) Calculate overall average rates and rate changes via **loss cost (pure premium) and loss ratio methods**
- The three reserving methods + pure-premium/loss-ratio ratemaking are [OFFICIAL] confirmed.
- Do NOT treat "average cost per claim" or "forms of experience rating" as FAM outcomes — those matched IFoA/UK course syllabi, not SOA FAM. [SECONDARY, disagreement noted]
- [UNVERIFIED] Whether earned/written/unearned premium and the parallelogram method have their own lettered bullet — implied by (c) and Friedland-based manuals, not quoted verbatim.

### Topic 6 — Option Pricing Fundamentals (2.5–7.5%)
Objective: "value simple options and derivatives using risk neutral expected present values" (binomial and Black-Scholes models). [OFFICIAL]
- Identify cash flows and characteristics of **puts and calls** (payoffs; options as insurance) [OFFICIAL]
- Apply the **binomial model** to price a simple **European-style** derivative on a single **non-dividend-paying** asset [OFFICIAL]
- Apply the **Black-Scholes formula** for the **price and delta hedge** of such a derivative [OFFICIAL]
- Apply **put-call parity** [OFFICIAL]
- No exotic options, no American early-exercise pricing, no dividends, no Greeks beyond delta appear in any source. [SECONDARY]

### Topic 7 — Long-Term Insurance Coverages and Retirement Financial Security Programs (2.5–5%)
Objective: "understand the key features of insurance coverages and retirement financial security programs." [OFFICIAL]
- a) Define and apply the concept of **insurable interest**. [OFFICIAL]
- b) Identify the long-term insurance coverages (life, health), annuities, and defined benefit / defined contribution pension plans. [OFFICIAL]
- Only these two bullets surfaced — the topic appears to have exactly two outcomes post-Nov-2024. Longer descriptive outcomes were removed Nov 2024.

### Topic 8 — Mortality Models (10–15%)
Objective: "understand key concepts concerning parametric and non-parametric mortality models for individual lives." [OFFICIAL]
Outcomes ([OFFICIAL/SECONDARY mix], each corroborated by ≥2 sources; lettering unconfirmed):
- a) Understand parametric survival models, life tables, and the relationships between them.
- b) Given a parametric survival model, calculate survival/mortality probabilities, force of mortality, curtate and complete moments of future lifetime.
- c) Identify and apply standard actuarial notation for future lifetime distributions and moments, including select and ultimate functions.
- d) Given a life table, calculate the same quantities, using appropriate **fractional age assumptions** (UDD, constant force) where necessary.
- e) Understand and apply **select life tables**.
- Fractional-age assumptions and select/ultimate tables are STILL IN for 2026. [OFFICIAL]

### Topic 9 — Present Value Random Variables for Long-Term Insurance Coverages
Objective: "perform calculations on the present value random variables associated with benefits and expenses for long term insurance coverages." [OFFICIAL]
- a) Identify PV random variables for life insurance, endowment, and annuity payments — **single lives**, annual / 1/m-thly / continuous frequencies. [OFFICIAL]
- b) Calculate probabilities, means, variances and **covariances**, using fractional-age or **claims acceleration** approximations where appropriate. [OFFICIAL] ([UNVERIFIED] whether "percentiles" appears in this bullet)
- c) Understand relationships between the insurance/endowment/annuity PV random variables and between their expected values. [OFFICIAL]
- d) Calculate the effect of changes in underlying assumptions (mortality, interest). [OFFICIAL]
- e) Identify and apply standard actuarial notation for the expected values in (a). [OFFICIAL]
- Recursion is NOT an explicit outcome bullet here. [SECONDARY — absence inferred]

### Topic 10 — Premium and Policy Value Calculation for Long-Term Insurance Coverages
Objective: "use and explain the premium and policy value calculation processes for long-term insurance coverages." [OFFICIAL]
- a) Identify future loss random variables for whole life, term life, and endowment insurance, and term and whole life annuities — single lives. [OFFICIAL]
- b) Calculate premiums by the **equivalence principle**, the **portfolio percentile principle**, and **for a given expected present value of profit**. [OFFICIAL]
- c) Calculate and interpret **gross premium, net premium, and modified net premium policy values** (modified reserves, e.g. FPT-style, ARE on FAM). [OFFICIAL]
- d) Calculate the effect of changes in underlying assumptions (mortality, interest). [OFFICIAL]
- "Expense reserves" and "recursion" are NOT named in the outcome bullets surfaced; both are covered in AMLCR readings and treated as examinable by study manuals. [UNVERIFIED as syllabus wording; SECONDARY support]

### Scope boundary: NOT on FAM (they are on ALTAM) [SECONDARY, consistent across all sources]
- Multiple-state models; multiple decrements; universal life; profit testing / profit analysis; joint life; pensions calculations. (FAM's "premium for a given EPV of profit" is a simple profit-loaded premium, not full profit testing.)

## 4. Explicitly OFF-Syllabus / Removed Material

All removals took effect with the **November 2024 sitting** (announced July 18, 2024, alongside the 40→34 question cut). No further removals or changes announced for any 2026 sitting, including fall/November 2026. [OFFICIAL for timing; item-level detail SECONDARY (ACTEX blog), strongly corroborated]

Removed effective Nov 2024:
- **Descriptions of the different types of short-term (P/C and health) insurance coverages** (Friedland-style coverage descriptions) — from old Topic 1.
- **Population mortality curves** ("identify common features of population mortality curves") — from the Mortality Models topic.
- **All of the old Topic 9 on mortality parametric AND non-parametric estimation** — Kaplan-Meier, Nelson-Aalen, and MLE for survival models are **no longer on FAM**. (The surviving "Parametric Estimation" topic is *loss-model* estimation, not mortality estimation.)
- **Tweedie distribution** and minor topics. [SECONDARY]

Added Nov 2024: **Loss Models §9.7** (effect of individual policy modifications on aggregate payments). [SECONDARY]

Never on FAM (ALTAM/ASTAM instead): Bühlmann/Bayesian credibility (ASTAM); multi-state, multiple decrement, UL, profit testing, joint life, pensions (ALTAM).

Note: one search synthesis mis-dated the removals to "November 2026" — the corroborated dating is **November 2024**.

## 7. FAM Tables & Notation (provided reference material)

- Single combined **"Tables for Exam FAM"** PDF (latest verified: 2024-10-exam-fam-tables.pdf); earlier split FAM-L/FAM-S versions exist. Content unchanged since 2022 per sources (2026-dated URL not directly verified). [OFFICIAL location / SECONDARY continuity]

- **FAM-S**: Loss Models Appendix A (continuous distributions) and Appendix B (discrete distributions) — parameterizations, moments; (a,b,0) family. Exact distribution inventory and whether VaR/TVaR formulas appear: [UNVERIFIED].
